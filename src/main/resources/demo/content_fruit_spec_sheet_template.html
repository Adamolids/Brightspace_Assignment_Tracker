Fruit Spec Sheet Template

Use this template for the spec sheet you submit.

  1. Specimen name, variety, and where it was sourced.
  2. Sugar content (Brix reading) taken from three samples, with the average.
  3. Firmness reading in Newtons, with a note on how it was measured.
  4. Two labelled photos: whole specimen and cross section.

Marking: accuracy of measurements 40%, photo quality 20%, slicing plan 40%.
